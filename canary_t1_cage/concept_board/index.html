Decided 2026-09-21 — structure = D, the 80/20 20-series frame

Recommendation to harden: D frame + A cartridges + bolted-and-dowelled IMU. A, B and C stay on the board as the printed-shell alternatives D was chosen over. The IMU is never on a sliding rail — in every concept it seats on a rigid keyed datum (the cyan block). Still open on D: the Core removal path is a hardening gate.

~0.42 kg · ~41 h
  • Loading. Peripherals drop in from the top between vertical 20-2020 mullions on printed T-keys; the battery slides in along X from the end; each cartridge held by one captive M5 thumbscrew into a slide-in T-nut.
  • Restraint. Belt / strap round the bottom long members. Rods are handle + antenna rail only, out of the restraint path.
  • IMU datum. 2 × M5 + a dowel into the extrusion cross member.
  • Thermal. Open top over the fan; printed louvered T-key inserts in the open slots (rear exhaust, −X intake).
  • T-key. Neck 5.6 / head 10.4 × 2.0 in an 11.0 cavity (cavity CONFIRM from the 80/20 CAD), 88 mm engaged.
  • Joints. M5 8.8 at 4.5 N·m + Loctite 243 + torque stripe.
  • Mass. ~4.5 kg gross vs the earlier 3.7 kg — 3.7 was the heaviest printed concept; ceiling to be restated by the owner. Load cases still close at 4.52 kg (18 g fwd = 798 N, rod MS 1.5).
  • Core. Opened often in T0 → first-class swap. Removal path not met as drawn: hardening gate.
