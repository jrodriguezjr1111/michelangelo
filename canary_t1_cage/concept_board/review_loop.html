THE EXCHANGE CONTRACT

What a STEP file has to be before either side trusts it. verify_step.py enforces this on every file in the folder; Andrew's exports get the same check on the way in.

One solidExactly one closed, valid solid per file. No loose bodies, no surfaces.
AnalyticPlanes, cylinders, cones. Zero tessellated or B-spline faces — it must mate, not just display.
MillimetresAP214, mm, origin at the centre of the mounting face, X = long axis, Z = up.
Provenance in the namePrefix tells you whether it was measured, estimated, or is a different product.
Envelopes, not partsNothing in the folder is a cage design. Section them, mate to them, keep out of them.

THE GATE

What station 03 runs on every STEP that comes back. Each item cites its requirement line so a fail is never a matter of taste. Every printed part in this repo that skipped one of these has failed on the bed.

Fit & clearance

  • Every envelope inside its bay with stated clearance; nothing over the Orin fan R4.2
  • Hole patterns land on the measured values, not the estimates
  • Ø8 driver column to every fastener with the part in place
  • IMU on a rigid keyed datum bolted and dowelled into the extrusion — never a rail, never a cartridge alone R3.1
  • Core (Orin + hub) has its own removal path: no neighbour out, no rod off R1.2

Loads & retention

  • 18 g forward case = 798 N at 4.5 kg into the frame's bottom long members; belt round metal, never a printed lug R2.1–2.3
  • Rods are handle + antenna rail only, out of the restraint path; 3 g swing case still closes R2.3, R7.2
  • T-slot joints M5 8.8 at 4.5 N·m + Loctite 243 + torque stripe; no preload on plastic R6.6
  • Printed T-key: neck 5.6, ≥ 88 mm engaged, layer lines along the neck; 11.0 cavity CONFIRM from the 80/20 CAD R6.5
  • Six-direction retention; battery latch and strap R2.4
  • Printed bearing ≤ 1 MPa; insert tension ≤ 50 N R2.5

Print & thermal

  • No printed part over 281 mm; frame members are cut to length, so the bed no longer sizes the structure R6.4
  • bottom_shell_layers ≤ 6 — the 30-layer raft that warped R6.3
  • max_bridge_length derived from the model's longest span — the 20 mm bridge that became spaghetti R6.3
  • ≥ 32 cm² intake and exhaust, battery out of the exhaust R4.2–4.3
  • PRINT_START temperatures verified literally in the gcode

IF YOU WANT TO CO-EDIT INSTEAD

Only if Javi wants his own hands on the model

The loop above is a review loop: one author, one checker, one decider. That is the right shape while Andrew is the CAD lead and Javi directs. If Javi wants to draw alongside Andrew on the same model, the tool changes:

  • Onshape — browser, runs on the Mac, real-time multi-user, versioning built in, SolidWorks users pick it up fast. Exports STEP/STL, so station 03 stays identical. Cost: a paid seat for private documents, and Andrew learning a second tool.
  • Not Fusion 360 here — no collaboration advantage over Onshape, and moving licensing.
  • Not FreeCAD — already on the Mac and scriptable, but a real step down from SolidWorks; Andrew would not thank you.

Either way, nothing about the exchange contract or the gate changes.
