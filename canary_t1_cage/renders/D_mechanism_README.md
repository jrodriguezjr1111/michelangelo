# Concept D — how the pull-out works (answers to Javi's questions)

**The rail is the T-slot itself.** No drawer slides, no printed rail: each cartridge has a printed T-key on each side wall (neck 5.6 wide, head 10.4 × 2, 88 mm long, 1 mm lead chamfers) that rides in the facing slots of two 20-2020 mullions; the cartridge drops in from the top, sits on the bottom long, and one captive M5 thumbscrew through the faceplate return into a slide-in T-nut in the mullion holds it up (`D_mechanism_sequence.png`, `D_mechanism_bay_cutaway.png`).
**Components sit on their own printed ASA tray, not a cheese board.** Each cartridge is one print: faceplate, side walls with keys, 3 mm floor, and four M3 heat-set standoffs on the component's *measured* pattern (FlyCatcher 58 × 49 drawn; ZED-F9P 37.6 sq, EG25-G 70.80 × 24.21, CO stick Ø4.216 hole, BNO085 19.95 × 17.81 are in `t1_params.py`) — a pattern-matched tray is stiffer, lighter, keys the board's orientation, and the cartridge *is* the module, so re-configuration means a different cartridge, not moving screws on a grid.
**Why concept B's 10 mm cheese grid was not carried into D:** the grid was B's answer to "everything on one plane, anything anywhere"; in D the frame's T-slots already give infinite positioning for anything frame-mounted, and inside a 74 mm cartridge a 10 mm grid cannot hit the measured patterns anyway (the brute-forced pitch in `tube_platform` was chosen for the *box*, not for a bay).
**Where a cheese-grid floor IS reasonable: the Core tray.** The UHR204 hub's mounting-ear pattern is not in its datasheet, so the Core tray floor (`D_mechanism_core_tray.png`, marker 6) carries a 10 mm M3 insert field under the hub; the Orin sits on its measured 91.86 × 58.37 pattern on a printed deck bridging over the hub. When the hub is measured, the field becomes four holes.
**The battery carrier is different:** it does not drop in — it slides along X on T-keys in the bottom longs' *inner* slots from the +X end (latch thumbscrew into a T-nut in the corner post + a strap round a frame member). The Core tray uses the same X-slide track from the −X end, which is why M03/M04 are cut 130 (5 mm gaps for the tray walls).
**The IMU is NOT a cartridge:** it bolts (2 × M5 + a dowel) into a keyed pocket in the rear aluminium rod-yoke plate on the top end tie — attitude reference on metal, never on a slide (R3.1).
**Drawn vs assumed:** drawn = the geometry above and the fixed 14-member frame; assumed = the 11.0 mm slot cavity (read the 80/20 CAD), the 44→70 mm cartridge depth (concept file draws 44 as massing), standoff height 6, the hub's outline and mass, the thumbscrew being a generic captive knurled M5×12. Nothing here is sliced or printed.
